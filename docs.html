Getting started

Everything here is built to order. The usual flow is simple: find a build in the shop or projects, place an order, and we confirm the details with you before anything is made.

  1. Browse the shop or projects.
  2. Send an order with your delivery location and payment choice.
  3. We reply to confirm specs, price, and timeline.

Ordering & delivery

Orders are placed through the form on the shop page. You choose one of two payment methods:

  • Cash on delivery — pay when the build arrives.
  • Request a payment link — we send a link once details are confirmed.

Your order details are emailed directly to us and are not stored in any database. Delivery times depend on the build and your location; we confirm an estimate before starting.

Setting up your build

Each build ships with setup notes specific to that board. General guidance:

  • Power the board only with the supply rating noted on its label.
  • For Wi-Fi builds, keep the network name and password handy for first setup.
  • Detailed wiring diagrams for each build will be added here over time.

FAQ

Can a build be customised?

Yes — most builds can be adapted (channel count, sensors, enclosure). Mention what you need in the order notes.

Do you store my personal information?

No. Orders are emailed to us and not saved to a database. A proper account system may be added later, only once it is properly secured.

What areas do you deliver to?

Get in touch with your location and we will confirm whether delivery is available and the estimated time.
