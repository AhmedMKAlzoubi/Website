Case variants

Card — the baseline

104.6 × 69.6 × 11.1 mm · 37.6 g

Two printed parts. The board drops into the tray from the front and rests on four ⌀4.5 mm bosses; the flat cover closes over it. One M2 screw per corner passes through cover, PCB and boss, so a single set of four screws clamps the whole stack.

The back is one unbroken surface — no pinholes, and the switches are sealed inside.

Desk nameplate — 18° lean

104.6 × 36.1 × 78.3 mm standing · 103 g

The card itself is unchanged — the plinth is fused onto the tray as one printed body, so there is nothing extra to assemble beyond the usual four screws.

It stands on its Ymax edge, which puts the USB-C port at the top where it stays reachable, with no cable tunnel needed. Screen centre sits 47.6 mm above the desk. Print it standing on the flat plinth base — no supports required.

Note: standing this way turns the board upside down relative to the card build, so the display driver needs a 180° rotation or the name comes out inverted. Nothing mechanical changes.

Conference badge — hangs either way

114.6 × 79.6 × 11.1 mm · 45.8 g

Same tray and cover as the card, plus an L-shaped flange wrapping the top and right edges. It sits centred in the 11.1 mm stack, so the badge hangs flat against the chest instead of tipping forward.

Four 15 × 3 mm obround slots let one badge hang both ways — the top pair (50 mm apart) for landscape, the side pair (30 mm apart) for portrait. Rounded ends are kinder to lanyard ribbon than square corners.

Landscape is the better hang. Portrait works, but the active area is 60.1 × 30.7 mm, so hung that way a name gets only 30.7 mm of width — and the closer side slots let it swing more.

Assembly

Screw stack-up
  • Head recess: ⌀4.2 × 1.2 counterbore
  • Through cover: ⌀2.4
  • Into boss: ⌀1.7 pilot, 4.5 deep
  • Usable shaft: 6.9 mm — use M2 × 6
Printing the case yourself

Cases come printed, but the models can be printed at home too. Orientation matters:

  • Card and badge: back face down.
  • Nameplate: standing on the plinth base.

None of the three need supports in the recommended orientation.

Ordering & delivery

How an order works
  1. Pick a variant in the shop and send the order form.
  2. We reply to confirm the details, the final price and a timeline.
  3. The board gets programmed and the case printed to your spec.
  4. It ships, and you pay on delivery or through a payment link.

Everything is made to order, so allow time for the build before delivery.

Payment and privacy

Two options: cash on delivery, or request a payment link which we send once details are confirmed.

Order details are emailed straight to us and are not stored in any database. There is no account to create, no card details are handled by this site, and nothing is tracked.

Care & charging

Day-to-day
  • Charge over USB-C on the top edge. Any normal phone charger will do.
  • The e-paper panel keeps its image with the power off, so the card still reads when the battery is flat.
  • The case is PLA. Keep it out of a hot parked car, which can soften the plastic.
  • Wipe with a dry cloth. It is not waterproof.

FAQ

Does the person tapping it need an app?

No. NFC is built into modern phones — they tap it and your details open. Nothing to install on either side.

Can I change what the card says later?

Yes. The card is re-programmable over USB-C. Get in touch and we will walk you through it.

Can I buy a spare case on its own?

Yes. All three variants share the same tray, cover and screw pattern, so a board bought today fits a shell printed later. Mention it in the order notes.

Do you deliver outside the UAE?

Get in touch with your location and we will confirm whether delivery is possible and what it would cost.

Do you store my personal information?

No. Orders arrive by email and live in that inbox only — there is no database behind this site. If accounts are ever added, it will be after the security work to do it properly.
