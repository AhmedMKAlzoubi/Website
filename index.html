Card drag me

01

Drawn in KiCad

Schematic, footprints and board layout from a blank sheet — not a reference design with a new silkscreen on top. Every trace placed on purpose.

02

Fabricated & assembled

Boards come back bare. Components go on by hand, then the panel, the antenna and the battery get fitted and checked.

03

Case modelled to fit

The shell is modelled around the finished board — 0.30 mm clearance, four ⌀4.5 bosses, and an NFC mark engraved 0.4 mm into the cover.

04

Printed & assembled

Tray and cover printed in your colour, board dropped in, one M2 screw per corner. No supports needed in the right orientation.

05

Programmed & sent

Your name, title and links written to the panel and the NFC tag. Then it ships — pay on delivery or by link.

02 — Under the hood

Small board, plenty going on.

Every millimetre had to earn its place at 11.1 mm thick.

Tap to share

NFC, aimed at exactly the right spot

The contactless mark is engraved 0.4 mm into the cover and runs up the antenna strip — so the symbol sits directly over the antenna it points at. Tap a phone, your details open. Nothing to install on either side.

Display
60.1

× 30.7 mm of active e-paper. Holds its image with the power off.

Sealed

No holes in the back

The rear face is one unbroken surface. Switches live inside, safe from pocket lint.

Power

USB-C and Li-Po

Charges from any phone charger. Battery pocket is 31 × 36 × 5.5 mm.

Serviceable

Four screws, that is it

One M2 through each corner clamps cover, board and boss. Opens as easily as it closes.

Made to order

Your name, your layout, your colour

Each card is programmed and printed per order. Tell us what it should say and which colour to print — the order form has a box for exactly that.

Open hardware

Yours to print

The case models are printable STLs, and all three variants share one tray, cover and screw pattern. Print a spare shell whenever you fancy a change.
