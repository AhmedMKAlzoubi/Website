What is inside

Designed in KiCad, assembled by hand, and built to survive a pocket.

Tap to share

NFC, pointed at the right spot

The contactless mark is engraved 0.4 mm into the cover and runs up the antenna strip — so the symbol sits directly over the antenna it points at. Tap a phone, your details open. No app to install.

Display
60.1

× 30.7 mm of active e-paper. Holds the last image with the power off.

Sealed

No holes in the back

The rear face is one unbroken surface. Switches sit inside, out of reach of pocket lint.

Power

USB-C and Li-Po

Charge over USB-C on the top edge. The battery pocket is 31 × 36 × 5.5 mm.

Serviceable

Four screws, that is all

One M2 through each corner clamps cover, board and boss together. Opens as easily as it closes.

Made to order

Your name, your layout

Every card is programmed and printed per order — name, title, links, and the case colour you choose. Tell us what it should say in the order notes.

Open hardware

Designed in KiCad

Board, footprints and enclosure were drawn from scratch — not a kit, not a reference design with a new silkscreen. The case models are printable STLs.
