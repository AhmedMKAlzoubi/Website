Build sequence

5 operations

What happens between your order arriving and the card reaching you.

Table 3 — Order of operations
OpOperationNotes
10Schematic & layoutDrawn in KiCad. Already done for stock variants; redrawn only for custom work.
20Fabricate & populateBare boards ordered, components placed and soldered by hand, panel and antenna fitted.
30Print enclosureTray and cover printed in the chosen colour. Card and badge print back-face down; nameplate stands on its plinth. No supports.
40AssembleBoard drops onto four ⌀4.5 bosses, cover closes, one M2 × 6 per corner clamps the stack.
50Programme & testYour name, title and links written to the panel and NFC tag, then tested against a phone before it ships.
