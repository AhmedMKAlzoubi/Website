Digital Card

A custom NFC business card: ESP32-C3, e-paper panel, USB-C charging and a Li-Po cell on a board drawn from scratch in KiCad. Three 3D-printed case variants share one tray, cover and screw pattern — card, desk nameplate and conference badge. The NFC mark is engraved into the cover directly over the antenna.
