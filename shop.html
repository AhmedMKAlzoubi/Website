2.1 — Card

Variant 2.1 · baseline

Card

Flat in the hand at 11.1 mm, with the back sealed as one unbroken surface — no pinholes, and the switches shut safely inside. The contactless mark is engraved 0.4 mm into the cover, running up the antenna strip so the symbol sits over the antenna it points at. This is the one for a wallet.

Variant 2.2 · standing

Desk nameplate

The same card with a rounded plinth fused onto the tray as a single printed body — nothing extra to assemble. It leans 18° from vertical, putting the screen centre 47.6 mm above the desk and the USB-C port on the top edge where it stays reachable.

2.3 — Badge

Variant 2.3 · hanging

Conference badge

An L-shaped flange wraps the top and right edges — right specifically, because the left wall carries the FPC exit slot. Four obround slots let one badge hang two ways: the top pair for landscape, the side pair for portrait. Landscape is the better hang.
